You need a straightforward way to stop juggling long hexadecimal addresses and use a single, memorable name across all Starknet dApps.

This guide walks you through the steps to transition from error-prone address copying to a simpler system using a .stark name that works universally across the ecosystem.

![A visual representation of simplified on-chain identity.](/blog/simplify-starknet-human-readable-addresses-img-1.webp)
*A simple on-chain name reduces the hassle of transactions*

## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure:

- You have an active Starknet wallet
- You have enough funds in your wallet for the registration fee
- You understand that .stark names are permanent once registered

## Step 1: Take Stock of Your Current Address Habits

Take a quick look at how you handle addresses now:

1. How many different Starknet addresses do you currently use?
2. Which dApps require you to enter your address manually?
3. Note any transaction errors you've had in the past 30 days.
4. Estimate the time you spend copying and verifying addresses each week.

If you're managing more than 3 addresses or spending over 10 minutes weekly on verification, it's time for Step 2.

## Step 2: Register Your .stark Name

Here's how to claim your name:

1. Visit https://starknet.id
2. Connect your Starknet wallet
3. Search for the .stark name you want
4. Check its availability and price
5. Complete the registration transaction
6. Confirm the new name appears in your wallet

**Checkpoint**: Your .stark name should resolve to your wallet address within 5 minutes.

![An image depicting human-readable crypto addresses.](/blog/simplify-starknet-human-readable-addresses-img-2.webp)
*Readable names help prevent copy-paste errors*

## Step 3: Connect Your Social Profiles

Strengthen your on-chain profile by linking other accounts:

1. Go to your StarknetID profile settings
2. Connect social media accounts (Twitter, Discord, GitHub)
3. Add a profile picture and description
4. Verify each connection through the platform's authentication process
5. Set your .stark name as your primary identifier

**Verification**: A green checkmark should appear next to each linked account in the StarknetID interface.

## Step 4: Use Your .stark Name in dApps

Now, replace those long addresses in your most-used applications:

1. List all the Starknet dApps you use
2. Update the profile settings in each dApp, replacing the old address with your .stark name
3. Test it out by sending a small transaction using your name instead of the hex address
4. Verify the transaction completes successfully
5. Keep a note of which dApps support .stark name resolution

If a dApp doesn't support .stark names yet, just continue using the full hex address for that specific application.

## Step 5: Adopt Your New Workflow

Make this your new routine:

1. Share your .stark name instead of long hex addresses
2. When sending funds, check if the recipient has a .stark name you can use
3. Use your name for all new dApp registrations
4. Monitor your transaction success rate over the next 30 days
5. Update any saved addresses in notes or other external systems

**Success Metric**: Your transaction error rate should drop to near zero within the first week.

## Embrace Your Simplified Starknet Identity

Your .stark name now acts as your universal Starknet passport. This system uses Starknet's native account abstraction to deliver a user-friendly experience like you're used to in Web2, but with the full security of Web3.

[Use your Starknet ID to prove your identity on-chain](https://starknet.id) and eliminate the complexity of managing addresses for good.
