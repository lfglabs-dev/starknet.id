Managing long, hexadecimal addresses on Starknet can lead to errors and confusion. A unified, memorable on-chain name solves this problem, making your crypto experience smoother and safer.

This guide provides the exact steps to create your Starknet ID and register a .stark name. Following this process will help you streamline your on-chain activity and prevent costly address-related mistakes.

![Starknet ID interface showing domain registration process](/blog/starknet-id-replace-complex-address-simple-stark-domain-img-1.webp)
*The Starknet ID interface, where you'll create your on-chain identity.*

## Step 1: Prepare Your Wallet and Funds

Before you can get started, you'll need to bridge some ETH over to Starknet.

**Prerequisites:**
- An active wallet for Starknet (Braavos, ArgentX, or compatible)
- ETH on Ethereum mainnet or a supported chain
- 0.009 to 0.248 ETH for your .stark name (price varies by length)

**Bridge ETH to Starknet:**
1. Use a bridge like Orbiter Finance or the official Starknet Bridge.
2. Connect the wallet that holds your ETH.
3. Select Starknet as the destination network.
4. Bridge at least 0.05 ETH to cover registration and gas fees.
5. Wait for the bridge confirmation, which usually takes 10-15 minutes.

**Verify the bridge was successful:**
- Check that your Starknet wallet balance reflects the bridged ETH.
- Confirm the transfer is visible on a Starknet block explorer.

## Step 2: Mint Your Free Starknet Identity NFT

Your first step is to mint a base identity before you can register a name.

1. Go to https://starknet.id.
2. Connect your Starknet wallet.
3. Click "Mint Identity" or "Create Identity."
4. Sign the request in your wallet.
5. Wait for the confirmation on the blockchain.

**Verification checkpoint:**
- You should now have a Starknet Identity NFT in your wallet.
- The NFT will appear in your wallet's NFT collection.
- The Starknet.id website should show your identity status as "Created."

## Step 3: Register Your .stark Name

Now it's time to choose and register your human-readable name.

**Name selection criteria:**
- 1-4 characters: 0.248 ETH (premium pricing to prevent squatting)
- 5+ characters: 0.009 ETH (standard pricing)
- Use only letters, numbers, and hyphens.
- Pick something memorable that's consistent with your brand or personality.

**How to Register:**
1. Enter your desired name in the search field.
2. Check its availability and price.
3. Click "Register" if it's available.
4. Review the registration fee and duration.
5. Sign the request in your wallet.
6. Wait for the blockchain confirmation.

**Post-registration verification:**
- Your new name will appear in your Starknet ID dashboard.
- Test it out by sharing it with another user.
- Confirm the name points to your wallet address.

## Step 4: Link Additional Identity Data

Connect your social accounts and other Web3 profiles to your Starknet ID.

**Available integrations:**
- Twitter/X accounts
- Discord profiles
- GitHub usernames
- Other Web3 names (ENS, etc.)
- Custom profile information

**Linking process:**
1. Access the "Profile" section in your Starknet ID dashboard.
2. Select the social platform you want to link.
3. Complete the verification steps for each platform.
4. Save your changes and sign the transaction with your wallet.
5. Verify the links appear correctly on your public profile.

## Step 5: Start Using Your New Identity

Start using your .stark name instead of your full hexadecimal address for all your Starknet activity.

**Immediate actions:**
- Update your address in DeFi protocols to your new .stark name.
- Share your .stark name instead of the long hex address when you need to receive funds.
- Add your .stark name to your social media bios.
- Test it out with a small transfer first.

**Verification protocol:**
- Send a small test amount using your .stark name as the recipient.
- Confirm the funds arrive in the correct wallet.
- Check that your name works correctly across different dApps.

**Common issues:**
- **DApp doesn't support .stark names:** If a dApp doesn't support them yet, you'll have to use your full hex address as a fallback.
- **Name not propagating:** If your name isn't working right away, wait 10-15 minutes for it to propagate across the network.
- **Transfer fails:** If a transfer fails, double-check that you have enough ETH in your wallet to cover gas fees.

Your Starknet ID and .stark name replace that complex address with something memorable, which helps reduce errors and gives you a unified on-chain presence.
